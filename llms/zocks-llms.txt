# Zocks

> Zocks is an AI assistant for financial advisors that captures client conversations and automates administrative work — meeting note-taking, meeting prep, form filling, client email drafting, document intelligence, and client profile management — and syncs the resulting client intelligence across CRM and wealth-management systems. Zocks exposes this client intelligence to AI tools through an official, OAuth-secured MCP server rather than a public REST API.

## Product
- [Zocks](https://www.zocks.io): AI assistant for financial advisors (note-taking, meeting prep, form fill, client email, document intelligence, client profiles).
- [Pricing](https://www.zocks.io/pricing): Essentials, Professional, Ultimate, and Enterprise Platform tiers; Zocks MCP is included from the Professional tier up.
- [Security](https://www.zocks.io/security): SOC 2 Type 2 certified; AWS as subprocessor; encryption at rest/in transit; annual pen testing.

## Agent access
- [Zocks MCP Server](https://help.zocks.io/en/collections/19244570-zocks-mcp-server): Hosted MCP server, OAuth 2.0 + Streamable HTTP, installed as a connector in Claude, ChatGPT, and Microsoft Copilot. Read access to meeting transcripts, summaries, results, conversation insights, and contact/household records. Tool-call-level audit logging.
